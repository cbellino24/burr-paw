FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers to what we hear most. Still stuck? Reach out anytime.

When will my order ship?

Usually within 1–2 business days. We are a small family business, so at times that can vary, but that is typical.

Is there a minimum quantity for custom logoing?

Yes. Custom logoing requires a minimum of 50 Burr Paws. They can be a mixture of colors — Black, Tan, and Orange.

How do I get a custom logo quote?

Custom logoing is quote-only and not available in the cart. Use the custom logo quote form with your quantity, color, and artwork notes.

Where can I buy Burr Paw in a store?

Use the dealer locator to search by city or zip. We’re expanding retail partners beyond Omaha — more locations coming soon.

What’s your return policy?

We stand behind the mitt. Review the refund policy for return windows and how to start a return.

How fast do you reply?

We typically respond within 1–2 business days. For the fastest path on branding orders, use the quote form; for everything else, call or email works great too.
