Custom logo questions

Quick answers about branding and quotes. Still stuck? Reach out anytime.

Is there a minimum quantity for custom logoing?

Yes. Custom logoing requires a minimum of 50 Burr Paws. They can be a mixture of colors — Black, Tan, and Orange.

How do I get a custom logo quote?

Custom logoing is quote-only and not available in the cart. Use the quote form above with your quantity, preferred color, and artwork notes.

How fast do you reply to quote requests?

We typically respond within 1–2 business days. We are a small family business, so at times that can vary, but that is typical.
